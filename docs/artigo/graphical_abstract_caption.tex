``A Two-Layer Deliberative--Reactive Architecture with a Threshold-Triggered
Attraction--Repulsion Layer for Substation Inspection''}
\end{center}
\vspace{1em}
\noindent
A two-layer architecture for substation inspection. A deliberative
active-sensing patrol (ROS~2, Nav2, MPPI) carries the sensors across the site;
a reactive layer, Stay Alert, attracts the robot to anomalies and
repels it from hazards. In simulation the fixed patrol inspects none;
Stay Alert inspects all three, with the clearance coming from the
keep-out, not the weighting.
